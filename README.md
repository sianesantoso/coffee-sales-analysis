# 📖 Project Overview

This project analyzes **coffee sales transactions** from **2019 to 2022** to evaluate business performance, identify sales trends, understand customer purchasing behavior, and uncover opportunities to improve profitability and customer retention.

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow using **Microsoft Excel** for data cleaning, exploratory analysis, and KPI calculations, while **Power BI** is used to develop an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Although the company consistently generates sales, management lacks clear visibility into product performance, customer purchasing patterns, and the effectiveness of its loyalty program.

A comprehensive analysis is required to identify profit drivers, improve customer retention, and support data-driven business decisions.

---

# ❓ Business Questions

- 📈 How have sales and profit changed from 2019 to 2022?
- ☕ Which coffee types and roast types generate the highest sales and profit?
- 👥 How effective is the loyalty program?
- 🛒 What are customers' purchasing behaviors?
- 🌎 Which countries contribute the highest revenue?
- 💡 What strategies can improve profitability and customer retention?

# 💡 Key Insights

### 📈 Sales & Profit Trend

- Sales reached their highest level in **2020**.
- **2021** generated the highest overall profitability.
- Both sales and profit declined in **2022**, indicating slower business performance.

---

### ☕ Product Performance

- **Arabica** was the most frequently ordered coffee type.
- **Liberica** generated the highest sales and overall profit.
- **Light Roast** delivered the highest profitability among roast types.

---

### 👥 Customer & Loyalty Analysis

- Loyalty members showed only a slight increase in average order value compared to non-members.
- Most customers made only a single purchase, indicating low customer retention.
- Increasing repeat purchases represents the greatest growth opportunity.

---

### 🌎 Geographic Performance

- The **United States** contributed the highest sales and profit.
- **Ireland** and the **United Kingdom** remained profitable secondary markets.

---

# 📈 Business Recommendations

- ✅ Promote high-margin products, especially **Liberica Light Roast**, through targeted marketing campaigns.
- ✅ Strengthen customer retention by encouraging repeat purchases with personalized promotions.
- ✅ Improve the loyalty program by offering more attractive rewards for frequent customers.
- ✅ Review pricing strategies and operational costs for lower-margin products.
- ✅ Expand marketing efforts in high-performing countries while exploring growth opportunities in emerging markets.
- ✅ Continuously monitor sales, profit, and customer retention to support sustainable business growth.
